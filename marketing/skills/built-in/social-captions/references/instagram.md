# Instagram caption

Part of [`social-captions`](../SKILL.md). The file `captions/instagram.txt` holds only the caption. Alt text for each image is in the campaign manifest; paste it in "Advanced settings, Write alt text".

## Template

```text
<A hook line: a question or a small truth the reader recognises.>

<2 or 3 short lines on what the feature does, in the reader's words.>

<The tagline.>

Explore <Product> on Google Play. Link in bio.

<3 to 8 hashtags>
```

## Rules

- The first line is all most people read before "more": make it worth stopping for (about 125 characters show).
- Links in captions are not clickable: say "Link in bio".
- Up to 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags are allowed; 3 to 8 specific hashtags work better. Put them at the end.
- A carousel gets one caption for the post. A reel puts its on-screen line in the first sentence too, since many watch muted.

## Example (Android Insider, Ask Studio Bot carousel)

```text
A good article can leave you with an even better question.

In Android Insider, tap Ask Studio Bot at the end of an article to bring that lesson into the chat. Choose a suggested prompt, edit it, or ask your own question. You decide when to send.

Read. Ask. Understand.

Explore Android Insider on Google Play. Link in bio.

#AndroidDevelopment #JetpackCompose #Kotlin #AndroidInsider #StudioBot

```
