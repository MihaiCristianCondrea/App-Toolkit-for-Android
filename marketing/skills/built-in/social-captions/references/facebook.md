# Facebook post

Part of [`social-captions`](../SKILL.md). The file `captions/facebook.txt` holds only the post.

## Template

```text
<A hook line.>

<2 or 3 sentences on what the product does and for whom.>

<Product> on Google Play: <store link>

<0 to 3 hashtags>
```

## Rules

- About 480 characters show before "See more". Keep the post shorter than that when possible.
- Links are clickable and get a preview card; one link.
- Hashtags matter little here: three at most.

## Example (Smart Cleaner)

```text
Running out of space again?

Smart Cleaner shows what is taking up your phone's storage and helps you clear it: large and forgotten files, duplicates, cache and photos you may not need.

Smart Cleaner on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.d4rk.cleaner

#SmartCleaner #Android
```
