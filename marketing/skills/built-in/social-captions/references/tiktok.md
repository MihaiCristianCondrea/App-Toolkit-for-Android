# TikTok caption

Part of [`social-captions`](../SKILL.md). The file `captions/tiktok.txt` holds only the caption.

## Template

```text
<One short hook line that matches the first second of the video.> <What the product does, in one sentence.> <3 to 5 hashtags>
```

## Rules

- Up to 4,000 characters, but only the first line shows over the video: keep the whole caption under about 150 characters.
- Links are not clickable: say "Link in bio" if needed.
- Hashtags: the product's own, the topic, and one broad discovery tag the audience uses.

## Example (Smart Cleaner)

```text
Your phone is full again. Smart Cleaner finds the big, forgotten and duplicate files in seconds. #SmartCleaner #PhoneTips #FreeUpSpace #Android
```
