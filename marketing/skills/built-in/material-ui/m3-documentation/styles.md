# Styles

## Styles

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/wT_VRcr1F26D9-YlbeUqii1zZjKYuRgGqEFVePdOcgRITVEEKLmd9sUCT13lORm18Pwl85M2UETDOy5_2OwJ9O9n8QirWi7nc5hPD5fSpRQIBBUi_PA>)

### Styles

Styles are the visual aspects of a UI that give it a distinct look and feel. They can be customized by changing your Material theme.

![Image](<https://kstatic.googleusercontent.com/files/41df9d7ab8c85c8f591046e88ccc069a1c986c1b01c2c8059c1aa91d658b2c58e8090f5f0b69759e3fd59d1847c91f654a24e7764d56f3b40636c8d49c05eab6>)

#### Color

Color is used to express style and communicate meaning. With dynamic color, Material puts personal color preferences and individual needs at the forefront of systematic color application.

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/2O88gXWwBFSkeBhCUnwCcdvB8fTi2gq3v4r4ddUADFRzDo2IAHxGJC886tgFstOjrK-IsBiQNZ3S4wRaf1YujU5K_-1qUTYFV8-dx9-c-IqP77tmMhCr>)

#### Elevation

Elevation is the relative distance between two surfaces along the z-axis. All surfaces and components have elevation values.

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/c7g7xi3GZwg6WaJAAG6zmGKgE5jH_dW0G_xCbgvKZvVeEc5i_Iv9QySokyDgZDnrvyYODZAj7viABV4JcPHnMON_dP-VrlzgO0sgy651xqG6o2lsTU35>)

#### Icons

Icons can be used to represent common actions. Material Symbols are a set of variable icon fonts created at seven weights across three different styles.

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/Ssx-uvsonXGkeVGgcxJyV5SERpNRbSRwB3VZEV-lfNW4x92lYlUNMdXO05Pk7gf0ZreLw_VTNToB2Uze9NYaatuKA7HWQjC8KK7MKg2NNXTCTIRiB67I>)

#### Motion

Use motion to make a UI expressive and easy to use. Material transitions help guide users as they navigate an app.

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/D1JcezVw2U7LIBF8FydwcCWvg0kBzHB9gvMzi0u9p2HEHmiPDoJ9xdBulMblLuDWx1zNlec7yV7B-iu3cFPZGIKIul3z0BD4FbzbkvyhaXFDyKJLdQ>)

#### Shape

The shape scale defines the style of container corners, offering a range of roundedness from square to fully circular.

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/PU1I3B2YbSWwjK535PVyoLgNKSUxgFceroFOVHmgWmZGko-1BN0b7Vw-g62SSBw4DEOR6s0pSKJ-eVqjOPHTlzAisZBZgDEC6IIr9d8L99dEgvQHqEs>)

#### Typography

Use typography to make writing legible and beautiful. Material's default type scale includes contrasting and flexible styles to support a wide range of use cases.

![Image](<https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/N8MooPZw0afInER0GAEg5XV6jCeHBTvDEzp8H9Sw3fgreoNxZWhVx5kTSuiqkvEedGrBCt-4YozvXIqPuWMKDHSqE6U4MVS1BWtj3amjYHFUGnHCZhw>)
