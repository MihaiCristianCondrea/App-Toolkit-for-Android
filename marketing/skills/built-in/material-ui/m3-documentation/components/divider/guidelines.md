# Divider

Dividers are thin lines that group content in lists or other containers

## Guidelines

![Inbox app using full-width dividers to separate messages.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6awcxp-1.png?alt=media&token=12fe2d89-f8a2-4225-af54-9e3ed1e5696a>)

_Full-width dividers_

## Usage

Dividers are one way to visually group components and create hierarchy. They can also be used to imply nested parent/child relationships.

The divider can be used in two ways: 

1.  Full width

2.  Inset

![Inbox app using full-width dividers to separate messages.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6ax8hs-2.png?alt=media&token=df5af949-7c28-4ff5-ae9d-2921bb69aad3>)

_Full-width divider_

![Music app using inset dividers to separate songs in a playlist.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6axm30-3.png?alt=media&token=8b4961e0-942d-44c1-ba45-c1c519b505d6>)

_Inset divider_

## Full-width dividers

Use full-width dividers to separate larger sections of unrelated content. They can be used directly on a surface or inside other components like cards or lists.

Full-width dividers can also separate interactive areas from non-interactive areas. 

They are used to group visual elements together, and indicate when elements are related to each other from an interaction perspective.

![Inbox messages visually separated by full-width dividers for improved readability.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6az64p-5.png?alt=media&token=8b64b7fe-bbf1-4a08-ae49-5d64147889e2>)

_Full-width dividers to indicate separation of content_

![Non-interactive information on a card and a clickable read more link separated by a full-width divider. ](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6azny5-6-do.png?alt=media&token=f0067f91-a5c2-4623-9abe-c9f2f6ef2c90>)

_Use full-width divider lines to separate interactive and non-interactive areas of a container such as a card_

![Full-width dividers used after every piece of content on a page.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b0986-7-caution.png?alt=media&token=d783f10a-ac99-47ae-93aa-30a247a78516>)

_Use full-width dividers sparingly. Too many divider lines will make an interface look cluttered._

## Inset dividers

Use inset dividers to separate related content within a section.

Inset dividers are equally indented from both sides of the screen by default.

![2 different content sections separated by an inset divider.  ](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b0tzg-8.png?alt=media&token=6f18c7dd-6395-4c8b-b986-995b10563a2a>)

_Inset dividers separate related content, such as emails in a list. They should be used with anchoring elements such as icons or avatars, and align with the leading edge of the screen._

![3 types of related content separated by inset dividers on a mobile screen.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b1g5j-9.png?alt=media&token=dcc21e9f-b77f-4d73-ad44-836bfcdc6e90>)

_Inset dividers in a list of related items_

![Inset divider separating body text and selection chips.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b1yh0-10.png?alt=media&token=e4fafa7d-57c0-4167-8d06-e572c40b6204>)

_Inset dividers can be placed in the middle of a layout to separate elements such as body text from selection chips_

### Using dividers both ways on the same screen

If dividers are used both ways in a UI, they must reinforce the hierarchy of information within different sections.

1.  To separate a different kind of content, use a full-width divider

2.  To separate nested content items, use inset dividers

![Dividers used to show content hierarchy: full-width for sections, inset for related items.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b2hjo-11-do.png?alt=media&token=fa232ddc-b5f2-4533-b074-c777e4e68607>)

_Use a combination of inset and full-width dividers to reflect the hierarchy of information_

List items with repetitive formats may not require an inset divider, in which using only the margin between items is acceptable.

![List with repetitive item formats. Visual separation achieved through margins only.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b329x-12-do.png?alt=media&token=1aefe556-bb41-4d12-b1da-7426eec7d7ae>)

_Content may not require a divider line_

## Vertical divider

A vertical divider can be used to arrange content on a larger screen, such as separating paragraph text from video or imagery media.

![Image and text side-by-side, separated by a vertical divider on a large screen.](<https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/design-spec/o/projects%2Fm3%2Fimages%2Flv6b3p0d-13.png?alt=media&token=19b15218-15a7-41dd-8a17-46f023941776>)
