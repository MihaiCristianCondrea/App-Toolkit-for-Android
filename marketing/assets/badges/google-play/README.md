# Get it on Google Play badge

`en_badge_web_generic.png` is Google's official English badge, downloaded unaltered from
<https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png> (646 × 250 px,
with its clear space built in).

Use it as Google's badge guidelines ask (<https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/visual-identity/badge-guidelines/>):
don't redraw, recolour, crop into or animate the artwork itself (scaling and moving it whole is fine), keep
its clear space, and keep it at least as prominent as needed to read. Where space permits, add the line
"Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC."
