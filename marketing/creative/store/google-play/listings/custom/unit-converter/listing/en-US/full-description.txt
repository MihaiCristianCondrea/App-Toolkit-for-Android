Everyday unit conversions in one place. App Toolkit's Unit Converter brings length, mass, temperature, volume and digital storage together in a clear interface. Choose two units, enter a value and read the conversion.

Length
Switch between metric and imperial lengths: millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres, inches, feet, yards and miles. Check familiar pairs such as metres and centimetres, or miles and kilometres.

Mass
Convert grams, kilograms, ounces, pounds and stone. Use the same two fields whether you start with a weight in kilograms or pounds.

Temperature
Convert Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin. For example, 20 degrees Celsius is 68 degrees Fahrenheit. Values below absolute zero are not accepted.

Volume
Work with millilitres, litres, cubic metres and US gallons. The gallon unit is US gallon, not imperial gallon.

Data
Convert bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes. These use decimal multiples: 1 GB equals 1000 MB. Binary units such as MiB and GiB are not included.

How it works
Open Measure in App Toolkit and select Unit Converter. Pick a category, then choose the From and To units. Edit either value to convert in the other direction, or tap the swap button to exchange the units.

More everyday tools
App Toolkit also includes Surface Ruler for on-screen lengths, Protractor for interactive angles and Tip & Bill Splitter for everyday calculations.

Five categories and 25 units cover everyday conversions. Results are a practical reference, not a promise of certified measurement precision.
