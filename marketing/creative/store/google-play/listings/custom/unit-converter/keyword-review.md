# Keyword review

213 English candidates, one per line in keywords.txt. All are unique ignoring case and no longer than 80 characters.

## Evidence and priority

1. Primary: unit converter, unit conversion, metric converter, and everyday unit converter.
2. Category and pair intent: length, mass and weight, temperature, volume, and decimal storage. Metres/centimetres, kg/lb, Celsius/Fahrenheit, litres/millilitres and GB/MB match the screenshots.
3. Secondary: other supported pairs, metric/imperial language, calculator wording for a numeric conversion, and bidirectional/swap intent.
4. Exploratory: British/American spellings, abbreviations and decimal storage queries. Storage queries can have binary-unit expectations, so review their relevance in Console.

These are candidate queries, not proven demand. Historical acquisition exports did not expose individual keyword volumes. The competitor review supports the use cases, not a forecast of rankings or installations.

## Rejected

Currency, exchange rates, crypto, area, speed, time, energy, pressure, power, cooking cups/spoons, data transfer rates, MiB/GiB, binary conversions, scientific calculators, saved history, favourites and camera measurement are outside this app's converter. Ambiguous gallon phrases were replaced with explicit US gallon terms. Competitor names are excluded.
