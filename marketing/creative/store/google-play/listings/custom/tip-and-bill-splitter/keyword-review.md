# Keyword review

Reviewed November 8, 2026. 189 English candidates, one per line in keywords.txt. These are targeting candidates, not disclosed search volumes or promised placements.

Primary: tip calculator, bill splitter, gratuity calculator, tip and bill splitter, restaurant tip calculator, per person bill calculator.

Secondary: equal restaurant and dinner splits, the supported 10/15/20 percent presets, group sizes and tip-inclusive totals.

Exploratory: common currency-code and denomination queries, natural-language questions, math/maths and number spelling variants. Currency queries mean calculating a bill already denominated in that currency. They do not mean converting money. Review broad terms such as restaurant calculator in Play Console before selecting them.

Rejected:
- Receipt scanner, OCR, itemized bill split and receipt tax extraction.
- Separate tip-per-person output, which the screen does not show, and awkward keyword permutations.
- Payment requests, restaurant payments, expense tracker, budget, shared ledger and cloud sync.
- Currency converter, exchange rates and travel tipping guides.
- Custom tip percentage, 5/12/18/25 percent tips and automatic tipping rules.
- No ads, no tracking, guaranteed accuracy, competitor names and ranking claims.

Evidence: public Google Play descriptions for [UKM APPS](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sanca.tip), [MCMLV1](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.mcmlv1.tipcalculator) and [TipSplit](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=za.co.protus.tipsplit). Their broader features inform the exclusions. Familiar use cases are research hypotheses. Historical App Toolkit exports did not disclose individually identifiable search queries.

Google Play Console decides which search terms are available and may group variants. No terms have been configured or confirmed there.
