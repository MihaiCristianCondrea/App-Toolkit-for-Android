# Ruler and Protractor custom listing

Status: live. Approved by Google Play review and published on November 8, 2026. The creative package uses the default listing's warm Material design language with one phone per screenshot.

## Audience and positioning

People looking for a screen ruler or touch-controlled protractor for everyday reference. Surface Ruler uses the display edge and offers scale calibration; Protractor selects an on-screen angle from 0 to 180 degrees. Measurements depend on the device and calibration. Neither tool provides camera, AR or professional measurements.

## Research

Reviewed November 7, 2026. [NixGame's Ruler](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.nixgame.ruler) describes on-screen measurement, calibration and a protractor. [EXA Tools' Protractor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.exatools.protractor) describes touch, camera and plumb modes. [Smart Protractor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.sira.protractor) describes touch, camera and sensor-based modes. App Toolkit's Protractor is touch-controlled; Bubble Level is a separate sensor-based tool.

The keyword list contains candidates, not measured search demand. Google Play Console determines which search terms and grouped variations are available for targeting.
