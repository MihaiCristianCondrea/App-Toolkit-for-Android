App Toolkit brings a screen ruler and an interactive protractor together for everyday measurements. Measure a small object against your phone's screen or set an angle by touch.

Surface Ruler

Use centimetre and millimetre or inch scales, or show both together. In Simple mode, drag the marker to read a length from zero. Advanced mode adds a two-point measurement range and settings for the scale edge, zero position and inch subdivisions.

Adjust the display scale against a physical ruler using the calibration control. Ruler layouts adapt to portrait and landscape screens.

Protractor

Set an angle from 0° to 180° by touching or dragging on the screen. The full-screen display shows the angle and its classification. Use the slider or the 0°, 45°, 90° and 180° presets to select an angle.

More measurement tools

Use Bubble Level to check your phone's tilt, or Unit Converter to convert between supported units of length and other quantities.

How to use

Open the Measure workspace and select Surface Ruler or Protractor. Place a small object against the ruler's screen edge, or use the protractor display to set an angle.

Accuracy and limitations

The ruler is limited to the size of your screen. Display characteristics vary by device, so compare its scale with a physical ruler and adjust calibration when needed. These tools are for everyday reference and do not guarantee measurement accuracy. The protractor is an on-screen tool and does not measure angles with the camera.
