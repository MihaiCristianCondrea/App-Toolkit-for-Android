Enter your choices. Spin the wheel. See what gets picked.

Decision Wheel in App Toolkit helps you make a small everyday choice when several options sound good. Add between 2 and 12 choices, one per line, then tap Spin the wheel. After the spin, the selected option appears below the wheel.

Your choices, your wheel
Choose a meal, pick an activity or decide who goes first. Try Pizza, Pasta, Sushi and Salad, or enter the names of a small group. You supply the options; the wheel makes a random pick.

More ways to choose
• Random Number: Set a whole-number range and generate a result within it.
• Coin Flip: Flip a virtual coin and see Heads or Tails.
• Dice Roll: Roll a virtual six-sided die.

Find the tools
Open App Toolkit and choose Play & Decide. Tap Decision Wheel to enter your options, or open one of the other chance tools.

The wheel supports 2 to 12 choices. These tools are for playful everyday decisions, not prize drawings or decisions that need a verified random process.
