# Keyword review

Search keywords for this custom listing live in `keywords.txt`, one per line, for Play Console's bulk add. The list is broad on purpose, for discovery research: it is not a claim that every phrase has search demand or improves ranking.

## What the data supports

Play Console's acquisition reports disclose only aggregate search-term categories, not individual keyword demand. English search intent comes first, and no term here is presented as having verified volume or a ranking benefit.

## Priority

1. **Established, direct:** spin the wheel, decision wheel, wheel spinner, random picker, random choice, decision maker, and their app and Android variants.
2. **Natural language and everyday uses:** help me decide, decide for me, what should I eat, who goes first, this or that, break a tie, food, activity, game night and date night pickers.
3. **The other chance tools:** random number (with the 1 to 2, 1 to 6, 1 to 10 and 1 to 100 presets), coin flip, dice roll, and the Coin Flip and Dice Roll Quick Settings tiles.
4. **Exploratory:** small-group and name pickers (the wheel takes 2 to 12 choices), yes or no, two-option picks.
5. **Brand:** App Toolkit with the wheel and each chance tool.

## Rejected

Rejected because they would bring people expecting something the app does not do, or name another product.

| Term | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| picker wheel | also the name of a well-known wheel web app |
| classroom names picker | classrooms usually exceed the wheel's 12-choice limit |
| classroom names wheel | classrooms usually exceed the wheel's 12-choice limit |
| choose a student from my list | classrooms usually exceed the wheel's 12-choice limit |
| classroom random picker | classrooms usually exceed the wheel's 12-choice limit |

Never add: raffle, lottery, giveaway or prize-draw terms (the description says the tools are not for prize drawings), gambling or roulette, team generators, list shufflers, "fair" or verified-random claims, large-group or classroom pickers beyond 12 choices, iPhone or iOS-only targeting, or other apps' and websites' names.
