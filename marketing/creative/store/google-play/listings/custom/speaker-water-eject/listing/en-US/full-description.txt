Water in your phone's speaker after a splash? App Toolkit includes Water Eject, a simple tool that plays a fixed 165 Hz speaker tone that may help dislodge droplets. Start a cycle lasting up to 30 seconds, follow the countdown and stop whenever you want.

Water Eject
• Fixed speaker tone: Play a 165 Hz sound through your phone's speaker.
• Timed cycle: See the time remaining during a cycle of up to 30 seconds.
• Stop anytime: Start and stop the tone using simple controls.
• On-screen guidance: Point the speaker downward and begin at a comfortable volume.

How to use
Open Quick Tools, select Water Eject and follow the on-screen guidance. Tap Start 30-second cycle to play the tone, or Stop cycle to end it early.

More than Water Eject
App Toolkit also includes:
• Compass and Bubble Level for direction and surface tilt.
• Ruler and Protractor for on-screen length and angle tools.
• Unit Converter and Tip & Bill Splitter for everyday calculations.
• Flash Dimmer and Morse/SOS light signals on supported hardware.
• Decision Wheel, Random Number, Coin Flip and Dice Roll.
• Counter, Reaction Test and a short guided Breathe pause.
Coin Flip, Counter, Dice Roll and Flash Dimmer can also be added as Android Quick Settings tiles. App Toolkit adapts its layout to phones, foldables and larger screens.

Important
The tone may help dislodge droplets near the speaker, but results vary by device and water exposure. It does not guarantee water removal, clean dust or debris, repair water damage or make your phone waterproof. Follow your device manufacturer's guidance after liquid exposure.
