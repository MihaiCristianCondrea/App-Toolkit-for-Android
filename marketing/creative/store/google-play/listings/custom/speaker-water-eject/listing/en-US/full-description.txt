Wet speaker after a splash? Water Eject in App Toolkit plays a 165 Hz speaker tone that may help dislodge droplets. Start a short cycle, watch the timer and stop whenever you need.

A simple speaker tone
Open Water Eject from Quick Tools. Point the speaker downwards and start at a comfortable volume. Tap Start 30-second cycle to play the tone. The cycle lasts up to 30 seconds, with a Stop cycle button available while it runs.

Know what it can do
Water Eject uses sound through your phone's speaker. It cannot guarantee water removal, repair water damage or make your phone waterproof. Follow your device manufacturer's guidance after water exposure.

Useful beyond the splash
App Toolkit also includes a compass, bubble level, flashlight controls, ruler and unit converter. Keep practical tools together in an app with layouts that adapt to phones and larger screens.
