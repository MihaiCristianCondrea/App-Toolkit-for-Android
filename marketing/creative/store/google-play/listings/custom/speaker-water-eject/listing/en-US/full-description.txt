Water in your phone's speaker after a splash? App Toolkit's Water Eject plays a 165 Hz tone that may help dislodge droplets. Start a short cycle, watch the countdown and stop whenever you need.

Water Eject is part of App Toolkit, a collection of everyday Android tools. After using the speaker tone, explore a compass, bubble level, ruler, unit converter and more in the same app.

Features

Water Eject for wet speakers
• 165 Hz tone: Play a fixed speaker tone that may help move water droplets.
• Timed cycle: Run the tone for up to 30 seconds and follow the countdown.
• Start and stop controls: Start when you are ready and stop the cycle at any time.
• Guidance: Point the speaker downwards and use a comfortable volume.

More everyday tools
• Compass and Bubble Level: Check your direction or a surface's tilt using your phone's sensors.
• Flash Dimmer and SOS: Control the flashlight and use light signals. Brightness adjustment depends on your device.
• Ruler and Protractor: Measure lengths on screen and check angles.
• Unit Converter and Tip & Bill Splitter: Convert units, calculate tips and split a bill.
• Play & Decide: Use a decision wheel, random number picker, coin flip or dice roll.

Benefits

• Simple controls: Open Water Eject, start the tone and stop when you want.
• Useful beyond a wet speaker: Keep measuring, calculation and decision tools together.
• Quick access: Use interactive widgets and add supported tools to Android Quick Settings.
• Adaptive design: Use App Toolkit on phones and larger screens.

How it works

Open Quick Tools and select Water Eject. Point your phone's speaker downwards, choose a comfortable volume and tap Start 30-second cycle. The countdown shows the remaining time; tap Stop cycle to end it early.

Water Eject may help dislodge droplets, but it cannot guarantee water removal, repair water damage or make your phone waterproof. Follow your device manufacturer's guidance after water exposure.
