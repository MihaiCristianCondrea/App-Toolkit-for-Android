Water in your phone's speaker after a splash? App Toolkit includes Water Eject, a tool that plays a fixed 165 Hz speaker tone that may help dislodge droplets. Start a cycle lasting up to 30 seconds, follow the countdown and stop whenever you want.

Water Eject is part of App Toolkit, a collection of everyday tools for measuring, calculating and making quick decisions.

Features

Water Eject
• Fixed speaker tone: Play a 165 Hz sound through your phone's speaker.
• Timed cycle: See the time remaining during a cycle of up to 30 seconds.
• Stop anytime: Start and stop the tone using simple controls.
• On-screen guidance: Point the speaker downward and begin at a comfortable volume.

How it works

Open Quick Tools, select Water Eject and follow the on-screen guidance. Point your speaker downward, start at a comfortable volume and tap Start 30-second cycle. The countdown shows the time remaining. Tap Stop cycle to end it early.

The tone may help dislodge droplets near the speaker, but results vary by device and water exposure. It does not guarantee water removal, clean dust or debris, repair water damage or make your phone waterproof. Follow your device manufacturer's guidance after liquid exposure.
