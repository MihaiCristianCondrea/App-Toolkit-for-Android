Water in your phone speaker? App Toolkit includes Water Eject, a simple speaker tone that may help dislodge droplets after a splash.

Open Water Eject, point the speaker downwards and start at a comfortable volume. The app plays a 165 Hz tone for up to 30 seconds. You can stop the cycle whenever you want.

A sound cannot guarantee water removal, repair water damage or make your phone waterproof. Follow your device manufacturer's guidance if your phone gets wet.
