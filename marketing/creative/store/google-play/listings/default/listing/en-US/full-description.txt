App Toolkit puts the small tools you reach for every day in one app: a compass and a bubble level, a ruler and a protractor on the screen, a unit converter, a tip and bill splitter, a counter, a flashlight you can dim, Morse and SOS signals, a decision wheel, coins and dice. Each tool opens in a sheet, and the ones Android supports can sit in your Quick Settings as tiles.

Quick Tools
The first tab shows live previews you can read at a glance, grouped by what they do.
• Compass: your heading from the phone's magnetic sensor, with the direction spelled out.
• Bubble Level: roll and pitch from the gravity sensor, to hang a frame or set a shelf straight.
• Flash Dimmer: turn the flashlight on and set its brightness, on phones whose torch can dim.
• Morse & SOS: flash the SOS pattern, or type a message and send it in Morse code with the torch.
• Water Eject: play a short low tone through the speaker after it gets wet. It can help shake droplets out; it does not repair water damage.
• Breathe: a short guided breathing pause.

Measure
Count, measure, convert and split, with each tool's latest result on the overview.
• Surface Ruler: measure small objects on the screen, in millimetres or inches.
• Protractor: lay the phone's edge along a line and drag the arm to read the angle, full screen.
• Unit Converter: length, weight, temperature, volume and data, both ways.
• Tip & Bill Splitter: the bill, the tip and each person's share, in the currency you choose.
• Counter: tap to add to a running count, with a minus button for a miscount.
• Reaction Test: wait for the signal, tap, and see your time against your best and your average.

Play & Decide
For the choices that don't need a meeting.
• Decision Wheel: type up to 12 choices, one per line, and spin.
• Random Number: a number between any two you set.
• Coin Flip and Dice Roll: tap the coin or the die right on the overview.

Made for every screen
App Toolkit follows Material 3 Expressive: it takes your wallpaper's colours, works in light and dark themes, and lays itself out for phones, foldables, tablets and Chromebooks, with a navigation rail or drawer on wide screens.

App Toolkit is free and open source. If something doesn't work as you expect, or you'd like a tool added, write a review with the details, or use Help & feedback in the app.
