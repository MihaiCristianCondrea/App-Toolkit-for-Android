I'm excited to present the official trailer for App Toolkit for Android. App Toolkit puts the small tools you reach for every day in one clean app. It helps you clear a wet speaker with Water Eject, measure lengths and angles with the Surface Ruler and Protractor, convert units, split a bill with tip included, spin the Decision Wheel, and open live sensor and signal tools whenever you need them.
