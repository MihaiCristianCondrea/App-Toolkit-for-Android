# Completed campaigns

Every campaign in this folder is published and finished. It is kept as a record of what was made and how.

- Do not modify, re-render or update these campaigns, unless the owner asks for one specifically.
- They may not follow later marketing skills, folder layout or tooling. That is expected: they are records of a
  campaign, not templates to keep up to date.
- To reuse one, copy what you need into a new campaign under `social-campaigns/<campaign-slug>/` and bring it up to
  date there.
