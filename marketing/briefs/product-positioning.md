# Product Positioning: App Toolkit for Android

## Core Value Proposition

App Toolkit for Android is a pocket utility and everyday companion app that combines Android Quick Settings tile shortcuts, interactive numerical tools, and a curated catalog of utilities by Mihai-Cristian Condrea.

## Primary Destinations

1. **Quick Tools**: Instant access to hardware sensors, display utilities, system shortcuts, and focus timers, each ready to open in an interactive sheet or pin to the Android Quick Settings shade.
2. **Numbers**: Dedicated destination to count, split, and convert with a live tally Counter, Tip & Bill Splitter, Unit Converter, Random Number Picker, and multi-sided Dice Roll.
3. **Apps**: Direct discovery of companion Android utilities and educational apps.
