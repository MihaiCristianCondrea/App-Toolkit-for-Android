# Product Positioning: App Toolkit for Android

## Core Value Proposition

App Toolkit for Android puts the small tools people reach for every day in one app: live sensor tools, on-screen
measuring, quick maths and a few ways to let chance decide. Each tool opens in a sheet, and the ones Android supports
can be pinned as Quick Settings tiles. No tool needs a sensitive permission.

## Primary Destinations

1. **Quick Tools**: live previews grouped by what they do. Sensors (Compass, Bubble Level), light and signals
   (Flash Dimmer, Morse & SOS), hardware and sound (Water Eject), and a short guided breathing pause (Breathe).
2. **Measure**: Surface Ruler, a full-screen Protractor, Unit Converter, Tip & Bill Splitter (with currencies),
   Counter and Reaction Test, each with its latest result on the overview, and a search.
3. **Play & Decide**: the Decision Wheel (up to 12 typed choices), Random Number, Coin Flip and Dice Roll, playable
   on the overview.

**Discover our apps**, in the drawer, is the catalog of the developer's other Android apps. Settings, Help & feedback,
Updates and Share come from the App Toolkit library.

## What not to claim

- Water Eject plays a tone that can help shake water out of the speaker; it does not repair water damage.
- No battery, speed or accuracy claims the app can't back, and no rankings or praise.
