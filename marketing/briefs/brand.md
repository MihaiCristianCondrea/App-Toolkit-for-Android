# Brand Brief: App Toolkit for Android

## Identity

* **Product Name**: App Toolkit for Android
* **Short Name**: App Toolkit
* **Developer**: Mihai-Cristian Condrea
* **Design System**: Material 3 Expressive with dynamic color palettes, Google Sans Flex typography, and distinct `MaterialShapes` polygon silhouettes.

## Visual Direction

* **Quick Tools**: Structured, collapsible categories for sensors, system toggles, and everyday utilities with direct Quick Settings tile integration.
* **Numbers**: Warm-gold surface palette (`#FFE082` to `#FFCA28` in light mode, `#5C4300` to `#362500` in dark mode) with grouped bento geometry (`28dp` outer corners, `8dp` inner seams) and distinct polygon icon badges (`SoftBurst`, `Cookie9Sided`, `Flower`, `Gem`, `Cookie12Sided`).
* **Apps**: Clean catalog presenting Mihai-Cristian Condrea's Android applications with category filters and Play Store links.
