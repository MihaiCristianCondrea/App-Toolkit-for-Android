# Brand Brief: App Toolkit for Android

## Identity

* **Product Name**: App Toolkit for Android
* **Short Name**: App Toolkit
* **Developer**: Mihai-Cristian Condrea
* **Design System**: Material 3 Expressive with dynamic color palettes, Google Sans Flex typography, and distinct `MaterialShapes` polygon silhouettes.

## Logo

* **Mark**: a toolbox with a wrench and a screwdriver, in ambers and cream (`.idea/icon.svg`; generated resources in
  `marketing/assets/logos/scripts`). The themed icon is the box with the tools cut out.

## Visual Direction

* **Quick Tools**: live widgets in `MaterialShapes`, grouped by category, each opening a sheet; supported tools pin to Quick Settings.
* **Measure**: warm-gold surface palette (`#FFE082` to `#FFCA28` in light mode, `#5C4300` to `#362500` in dark mode) with grouped bento geometry (`28dp` outer corners, `8dp` inner seams), live previews of each tool and polygon icon badges (`SoftBurst`, `Cookie9Sided`, `Flower`, `Gem`, `Cookie12Sided`).
* **Play & Decide**: the Decision Wheel card and the chance tools (random number, coin, dice) in the same gold group.
* **Store and campaigns**: the toolbox's ambers for grounds and shapes (`#FFF4D9` ground, `#8F5700` caption pill), with the app's screens in their own colours.
