## Influences without a license

Two other skills influenced this one. Neither published a license, so no text from them is
reproduced. Their ideas were rewritten in new prose and checked against the official documentation.

- gdlbo, material-skill (https://github.com/gdlbo/material-skill). No license file and no license
  terms in its README, checked 2026-11-20. Influenced the design-quality, accessibility, adaptive,
  Android, web, and audit guidance.

  `official/google/material-ui/m3`. Its origin and license could not be verified. Influenced the
  theming, web setup, adaptive layout, component index, and audit procedure.

## Accuracy

The documentation snapshot reflects m3.material.io on 2026-05-21, the images July 2026, and the
library status 2026-11-20. Material and its libraries change after those dates. Verify against the
installed libraries and current release notes before relying on version-sensitive facts, and use the
scripts in `scripts/` to check for updates. No warranty is made that this skill matches the current
state of Material Design.
